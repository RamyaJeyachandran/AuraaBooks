Gmail SMTP Setup Instructions

  • Set Host to smtp.gmail.com
  • Set Connection to TLS (Port: 587) or SSL (Port: 465).
  • Ensure 2-Step Verification is enabled in your Google Account.
  • Create an App Password in your Google Account Security section, and paste it into the Password field instead of your main password.
